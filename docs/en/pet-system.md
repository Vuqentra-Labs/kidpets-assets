## Overview

The Pet System is designed around meaningful choices. Every Pet has a distinct identity and should offer a different way to play.

A Pet may improve combat, defense, exploration, mining, water survival, food management or another specific gameplay area.

## Core Rules

1. No two Pets should have the same ability.
2. Every ability must make sense for the selected creature.
3. Every Pet must include all four Pet qualities.
4. Stronger abilities must have meaningful cooldowns, charging requirements or limitations.
5. Pet progression should be connected to using that Pet correctly.

## Four Pet Qualities

### Action Passive

A small and frequent effect triggered by a related player action.

Examples:

- Mining may grant one additional resource.
- Gathering plants may grant additional natural drops.
- Fighting may charge an offensive effect.

### Need Passive

An automatic effect triggered when the player needs assistance.

Examples:

- Activates when hunger is low.
- Activates when health is low.
- Activates when oxygen is almost depleted.

### Active Ability

A manually triggered ability with a moderate effect and cooldown.

Examples:

- Immediate healing.
- Resource detection.
- Temporary food assistance.

### Ultimate Ability

A powerful ability with a long cooldown or charging mechanic.

Examples:

- Strong emergency healing.
- Revealing valuable resources in a large area.
- Producing a rare item.

## Progression

Pet progression should require both experience and Pet-specific achievements.

Example:

- The Axolotl gains experience by healing.
- The Sniffer gains experience through natural gathering.
- The Bat gains experience through mining and cave exploration.

A Pet should only level up when all configured requirements are completed.

## Balance Principles

- Frequent passives should provide small benefits.
- Need Passives must have cooldowns.
- Active Abilities should be useful without replacing the entire gameplay loop.
- Ultimate Abilities should feel powerful but remain limited.
- Pets should have strengths and weaknesses.
