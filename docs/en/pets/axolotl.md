# Axolotl

## Overview

| Information | Value |
|---|---|
| Main Role | Healing |
| Secondary Role | Emergency Survival |
| Recommended Environment | General gameplay and water areas |
| Playstyle | Support and recovery |

The Axolotl is focused on keeping the player alive through immediate healing, regeneration and emergency support.

## Action Passive — Restorative Bond

Healing performed or received during eligible actions charges the Axolotl's restorative energy.

**Possible triggers:** successful healing and configured recovery events.  
**Recommended limitation:** prevent repeated artificial healing loops.

## Need Passive — Emergency Regeneration

When the player's health falls below a configured threshold, the Axolotl applies temporary regeneration.

**Recommended limitation:** meaningful cooldown.

## Active Ability — Healing Pulse

The Axolotl immediately restores a configured amount of health.

**Recommended limitation:** moderate cooldown and no activation at full health.

## Ultimate Ability — Vital Tide

The Axolotl releases a strong restorative wave, providing major healing and optionally removing configured negative effects.

**Recommended limitation:** long cooldown or restorative-energy charge requirement.

## Progression Ideas

| Level Requirement | Example |
|---|---|
| Healing | Restore a required number of hearts |
| Emergency Support | Trigger Emergency Regeneration |
| Active Usage | Use Healing Pulse effectively |
| Ultimate Mastery | Use Vital Tide during dangerous situations |
