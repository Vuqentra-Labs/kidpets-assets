# Bat

## Overview

| Information | Value |
|---|---|
| Main Role | Mining |
| Secondary Role | Cave Exploration |
| Recommended Environment | Caves and underground areas |
| Playstyle | Resource gathering and navigation |

The Bat specializes in underground exploration, mining assistance and finding valuable resources.

## Action Passive — Mineral Echo

When the player mines eligible naturally generated blocks, the Bat has a chance to provide an additional resource.

**Recommended limitation:** player-placed blocks should not count.

## Need Passive — Cave Sight

When the player remains in low light, the Bat provides temporary visual assistance.

**Recommended limitation:** short duration and cooldown.

## Active Ability — Echolocation

The Bat scans the nearby area and indicates the direction of eligible resources or cave paths.

**Recommended limitation:** moderate radius and cooldown.

## Ultimate Ability — Deep Resonance

The Bat performs a powerful underground scan, revealing valuable configured resources in a larger area.

**Recommended limitation:** long cooldown, limited duration and restricted resource list.

## Progression Ideas

| Level Requirement | Example |
|---|---|
| Mining | Mine naturally generated blocks |
| Ore Discovery | Discover configured ores |
| Cave Exploration | Travel through underground areas |
| Active Usage | Successfully use Echolocation |
