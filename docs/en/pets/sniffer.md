# Sniffer

## Overview

| Information | Value |
|---|---|
| Main Role | Gathering and Nature |
| Secondary Role | Food and Survival |
| Recommended Environment | Forests, plains and farms |
| Playstyle | Gathering and exploration |

The Sniffer helps players interact with nature, gather additional resources and handle food-related needs.

## Action Passive — Natural Instinct

When the player gathers eligible natural resources, the Sniffer has a chance to provide an additional related drop.

**Possible triggers:** grass, leaves, flowers and configured plants.  
**Recommended limitation:** player-placed blocks should not count.

## Need Passive — Emergency Foraging

When the player's hunger becomes low, the Sniffer may find simple food.

**Trigger:** configured hunger threshold.  
**Recommended limitation:** cooldown and restricted food list.

## Active Ability — Resource Scent

The Sniffer searches the nearby area and points toward an eligible natural resource.

**Recommended use:** exploration and gathering assistance.  
**Recommended limitation:** moderate cooldown and detection radius.

## Ultimate Ability — Ancient Dig

The Sniffer excavates a rare natural reward from a configured reward table.

**Recommended limitation:** long cooldown, charge requirement or biome restrictions.

## Progression Ideas

| Level Requirement | Example |
|---|---|
| Gathering | Collect natural plants |
| Food Support | Trigger Emergency Foraging |
| Exploration | Discover configured biomes |
| Active Usage | Use Resource Scent successfully |
